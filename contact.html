Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our solutions and services

What is the implementation timeline?

Most implementations take 8-12 weeks depending on solution complexity and customization requirements. Standard solutions can be deployed in as little as 4 weeks.

Do you offer cloud or on-premise deployment?

We offer both deployment options. Cloud deployment is recommended for faster implementation, scalability, and lower maintenance costs. On-premise is available for institutions with specific security requirements.

What support do you provide after implementation?

We provide 24/7 technical support, regular system updates, security patches, dedicated account management, and ongoing training for your team.

Can we customize the solutions?

Yes, all our solutions can be customized to meet your specific business requirements, local regulations, and unique workflows.

What security standards do you follow?

We implement bank-grade security with encryption, multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, and compliance with local and international regulations.

Do you provide training?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for administrators, tellers, and end-users, including documentation, video tutorials, and on-site or remote training sessions.
